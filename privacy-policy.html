Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Overview

Hover Image Saver is designed to help users download images from websites using customizable shortcuts and download settings. This extension is designed with privacy in mind and does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information.

Information

Hover Image Saver does NOT collect:

  • Personal information
  • Email addresses
  • Passwords
  • Browsing history
  • Payment information
  • Account credentials
  • Downloaded image contents

Local Storage

The extension stores user preferences locally using the browser's storage system. Examples include:

  • Hotkey settings
  • Download preferences
  • Folder templates
  • Whitelist and blacklist settings
  • Notification preferences

These settings remain on the user's device and are not transmitted to the developer.

Downloads Permission

The Downloads permission is used solely to allow the extension to save images that the user explicitly chooses to download. The extension does not automatically upload, share, or transmit downloaded files.

Website Access

The extension may access web pages in order to detect images and perform image downloads requested by the user. No website content is transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Links

The extension may contain links to third-party websites such as PayPal or feedback forms. These websites operate under their own privacy policies.

Changes to this Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect new features or improvements.

Contact

Questions, feedback, or privacy concerns may be submitted through the feedback form provided within the extension.
